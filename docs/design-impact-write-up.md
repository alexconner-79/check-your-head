# design-impact-write-up

Turns a piece of design work into a credible account of what it achieved, from whatever evidence you have. It never invents or rounds up numbers.

## When to use it

- A project has ended and stakeholders want to know what changed
- You need evidence for a performance review
- Your portfolio needs a case study or a short summary
- You have strong work but thin numbers and need to present it honestly

## How to ask

- "Write up the impact of the payee setup redesign"
- "Turn this project into a portfolio case study"
- "Give me a 150-word summary of this work for my CV"

Give it whatever you have: notes, Figma links, tickets, Slack threads, metrics, decision records, or just a description.

## Formats

- **Impact write-up**: internal, about 400 words
- **Portfolio case study**: public-facing, with prompts for what to show at each stage
- **Portfolio summary**: about 150 words, always included with the longer formats

All follow the same order: problem, your role, key decisions and their trade-offs, outcome (including what didn't move), and what it made possible. Each ends with **evidence gaps**: what would strengthen it and where to get it.

See the worked example: [payee setup](../skills/design-impact-write-up/examples/payee-setup.md).

## Honesty rules

The [evidence ladder](../skills/design-impact-write-up/references/evidence-ladder.md) ranks evidence from A/B tests down to anecdote, and gives wording that matches the strength of each claim. For example, "completion rose from 41% to 52% after launch, alongside a pricing change" rather than "design drove an 11 point rise".

Every figure is labelled measured, proxy or estimate. Your role is described precisely: "led", "designed" and "contributed to" mean different things. Strategic and 0-to-1 work is described as such, with outcomes like a launch, an approval or team adoption where percentages don't apply.

If the work is confidential, it asks whether to anonymise the client, product and figures first.

## Tools

| Reads from | Writes to |
|---|---|
| Decision records, metrics plans, debt registers, Figma, tickets, notes | A Claude doc, a Notion or Confluence page, or a repo file at `docs/case-studies/` |
