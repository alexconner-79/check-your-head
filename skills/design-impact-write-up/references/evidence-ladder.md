# Evidence ladder

Strongest first. Use the strongest available, and say which rung each claim sits on.

| Rung | Type | Example | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Controlled experiment | A/B test with adequate sample | measured (A/B) |
| 2 | Before and after against a baseline | Completion 41% before, 52% six weeks after | measured |
| 3 | Proxy metric | Contacts about the journey down 30% | proxy |
| 4 | Usability benchmark | Task success 3/6 before, 6/6 after | measured (usability, small sample) |
| 5 | Adoption and reuse | Pattern adopted by 4 other squads | measured |
| 6 | Qualitative | Stakeholder quotes, research findings, feedback themes | qualitative |
| 7 | Estimate | Ops estimate of calls avoided | estimated |
| 8 | Anecdote | One customer's comment | qualitative (single) |

## Contribution vs attribution

Use language that matches the strength of the link.

| Evidence | Say | Don't say |
|---|---|---|
| A/B test | "The redesign increased completion by 11 points" | |
| Before and after, nothing else changed | "After the redesign, completion rose from 41% to 52%" | "I increased completion by 27%" |
| Before and after, other changes too | "Completion rose from 41% to 52% after launch, alongside a pricing change" | "The design drove an 11 point rise" |
| Proxy | "Onboarding contacts fell 30% in the following quarter (proxy)" | "Saved £200k in call costs" (unless finance calculated it) |
| No data | "Adopted as the standard pattern by four squads" | Any number |

Absolute and relative change: state both, or say which. "Up 11 points (41% to 52%)" is clearer than "up 27%".

## When outcomes didn't move

Say so. Then say why, if known: shipped late, measurement not in place, constrained scope, a confounding change. A write-up that admits a miss is more credible than one that doesn't.

## Strategic and 0-to-1 work

Outcomes may be capability, not metrics: a product that now exists, a design system teams build on, a regulatory approval, a funding round the prototype supported. These are legitimate outcomes. Evidence them with what happened next (adoption, launch, approval) rather than forcing a percentage.

## Confidentiality

When anonymising: replace client with sector and size ("a UK challenger bank"), round figures only if the user asks and label them "rounded", and remove internal names.
