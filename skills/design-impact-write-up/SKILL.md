# Design Impact Write-Up

Turn a piece of design work into a credible account of its impact. Works from whatever exists: notes, Figma files, tickets, Slack threads, decision records, metrics plans, debt registers, a verbal description. It does not need any earlier record to have been kept.

## Formats

Pick from the request, or ask once if unclear:

- **Impact write-up**: internal, for stakeholders or a performance review. About 400 words.
- **Portfolio case study**: public-facing, longer narrative, with what to show at each stage.
- **Portfolio summary**: about 150 words, for a card, CV entry or intro.

Always produce the 150-word summary alongside either of the longer formats.

## Inputs

Read `references/tool-routing.md` for sources. If the routing note lists decision records, metrics plans or a debt register for the project, read them; they are the best raw material.

## Process

1. **Gather** what exists. Check confidentiality: if the work is under NDA or client-confidential, ask whether to anonymise the client, product and figures before writing.
2. **Sort the evidence** using the ladder in `references/evidence-ladder.md`: measured, proxy, qualitative, missing.
3. **Ask once** for the most valuable missing item if outcome evidence is thin (usually a before figure, or what changed after launch). Then write with what exists rather than stalling.
4. **Write** in the chosen format from `templates/`.
5. **List evidence gaps** that would materially strengthen it and could still be obtained.

## Structure

1. **Problem**: what was wrong, for whom, and what it cost in the business's terms.
2. **My role**: what the user specifically did and owned, separate from the team.
3. **Key decisions**: three at most, each with the trade-off involved.
4. **Outcome**: results against baseline, most significant first. Then what did not move, and why.
5. **What it made possible**: reuse, design system adoption, delivery speed, new capability.

See `examples/payee-setup.md`.

## Rules

- **Never invent or round up numbers.** Label every figure: measured, estimated or proxy.
- **Separate contribution from attribution** using the language in `references/evidence-ladder.md`.
- **Frame strategic, 0-to-1 and redesign work as such.** Don't shrink it into delivery language.
- **Be precise about role.** "I led", "I designed", "I contributed to" mean different things; use the one that's true.
- Use qualitative evidence when there are no numbers, and label it plainly.
- No inflated verbs: spearheaded, revolutionised, drove, transformed, championed.
- No em dashes, no filler.
- For a write-up, deliver as a document (Docs artifact, Notion page or markdown, per routing), not just chat.
