# Pattern catalogue

Grouped by where they usually appear. "Signals" are what to look for in designs, copy or code.

## Consent and privacy

| Pattern | What it is | Signals |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-ticked consent | Opt-in already selected | `checked`, `defaultChecked`, `true` defaults on marketing or data consent |
| Bundled consent | Several purposes behind one tick | One checkbox covering marketing, partners and profiling |
| Asymmetric choice | Accept prominent, reject hidden or multi-step | Primary button for Accept, text link or second layer for Reject |
| Consent wall | Access blocked until consent given, where not necessary | Modal with no dismiss or reject |
| Privacy zuckering | Settings that default to maximum sharing, hard to find | Sharing toggles on by default, buried in settings |

## Pricing

| Pattern | What it is | Signals |
|---|---|---|
| Drip pricing | Mandatory fees revealed late in the journey | Price in step one differs from final total; fees added at checkout |
| Hidden costs | Charges not clearly shown before commitment | Fees in small print, tooltips or T&Cs only |
| Misleading reference price | Inflated "was" price | Was/now with no evidence the was price was charged |
| Unclear price rise | Increases framed vaguely or hidden | Percentages or "CPI plus" instead of amounts; notices buried in email |
| Bait and switch | Advertised option unavailable or different | "From" prices nobody qualifies for |

## Subscriptions

| Pattern | What it is | Signals |
|---|---|---|
| Roach motel | Easy to join, hard to leave | Online sign-up, phone-only cancel; cancel link hidden in settings |
| Forced continuity | Trial rolls into paid without clear notice | No reminder before charge; renewal terms in small print |
| Save-offer loop | Repeated retention screens before cancelling | Three or more screens between "Cancel" and confirmation |
| Silent auto-renewal | Renews without reminder | No pre-renewal notice |

## Pressure

| Pattern | What it is | Signals |
|---|---|---|
| Fake urgency | Deadlines that aren't real | Countdown timers that reset on reload, "offer ends today" every day |
| Fake scarcity | Stock or demand claims that aren't true | "Only 2 left", "12 people viewing" with no data source |
| Confirmshaming | Guilt-laden decline option | "No thanks, I don't like saving money" |
| Social proof inflation | Exaggerated or invented popularity | Round counts with no source, fabricated reviews |

## Interface interference

| Pattern | What it is | Signals |
|---|---|---|
| Visual misdirection | Hierarchy steers the business-preferred choice | Preferred option styled primary regardless of user intent |
| Trick questions | Confusing wording, double negatives | "Untick to not receive" |
| Disguised ads | Ads styled as content or controls | Sponsored items without clear labels; fake download buttons |
| Sneak into basket | Items or insurance added by default | Add-ons pre-selected in checkout |
| Nagging | Repeated prompts after a decline | Same modal on every visit |
| Obstruction | User-favourable path made longer | Extra steps, re-authentication or calls to downgrade |

## AI and chatbot patterns

Increasingly relevant as products add assistants and agents. Check these whenever there is a conversational or agentic interface.

| Pattern | What it is | Signals |
|---|---|---|
| False intimacy | Relationship language used to drive purchases or retention | Assistant expresses feelings, loneliness or disappointment when the user leaves or declines |
| Guilt on exit | Emotional pressure when ending a session or cancelling | "Are you sure you want to leave me?" |
| Undisclosed persuasion | Personalised persuasion presented as neutral advice | Recommendations steered to paid or partner products without disclosure |
| Opaque memory and data use | Conversation data kept or reused without clear notice or control | No visible memory controls; training use buried in policy |
| Sycophantic agreement | Agreeing to keep the user engaged rather than being accurate | Assistant validates risky choices tied to revenue |
| Hidden human escalation | Hard to reach a person when needed | No route to a human in support or complaints flows |
| Anthropomorphic cover | Presenting a bot as a person | Human names and photos with no AI disclosure |

## Usually legitimate

Say so when you see these, unless the execution tips them over:

- A genuine, time-limited offer with a real end date
- One clear retention offer during cancellation, with an equally clear "continue cancelling"
- Defaults that suit most users and are easy to change
- Honest social proof with a source
- Reminders the user opted into
